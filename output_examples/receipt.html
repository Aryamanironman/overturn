Why the denial is wrong

DEN-01 · rebutted

The service is experimental or investigational.

The denial cites POL-4.3, the experimental exclusion. That clause applies to SPINE-FUSION-001 and states it does not apply to diagnostic MRI of the lumbar spine. POL-4.1 covers MRI-LS-001 after at least six weeks of conservative therapy.
sources: policy_clauses:POL-4.3, policy_clauses:POL-4.1, medical_records:REC-016, eob:EOB-001
DEN-02 · rebutted

Procedure code MRI-LS-002 (MRI with contrast) is not covered without prior authorisation.

The denial cites MRI-LS-002 and diagnosis M51.1. The order, the EOB and the record use MRI-LS-001 and diagnosis M54.5. MRI-LS-001 is described as 'MRI lumbar spine without contrast' and does not require prior authorisation; MRI-LS-002 is the contrast study that does.
sources: procedure_codes:MRI-LS-002, procedure_codes:MRI-LS-001, eob:EOB-001, medical_records:REC-016, icd10_codes:M51.1, icd10_codes:M54.5
DEN-03 · rebutted

No conservative treatment is documented.

The record documents 12 physiotherapy sessions over 12 weeks and an NSAID trial. POL-5.1 requires at least six weeks of conservative therapy.
